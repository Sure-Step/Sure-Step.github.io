Problem

As medical technology saves more lives both young and old, those with chronic conditions are in need of more care than ever before. More people are finding themselves in the position of informal caregiver, professional caregivers are dealing with an increased load of patients, and both are left with little to no help from others and a lack of purpose-built tools.

Characteristics

Caregiver burden, which plagues nearly 50% of informal caregivers (Soh et al., 2025), is primarily linked to insufficient resources and responsibility conflict (Liu et al., 2020).

More than 1 in 5 Americans are caregivers, having provided care to an adult or child with special needs within the past year (AARP, 2020).

For informal caregivers of family dealing with chronic or rare conditions, proactive planning was the greatest reducer of stress (Zajdel et al., 2023).

More than half of all caregivers need more information and help with care topics such as managing stress and keeping their patient safe (AARP, 2020).
