Problem

As medical technology saves more lives both young and old, those with chronic conditions are in need of more care than ever before. Professional caregivers are in need of better tools to handle increased load, and more non-professionals are finding themselves in the position of informal caregiver.

Characteristics

The state of care

Caregiver burden, which plagues nearly 50% of informal caregivers [3], is primarily linked to insufficient financial resources and responsibility conflict [2]

For informal caregivers of family dealing with chronic or rare conditions, proactive planning was the greatest reducer of stress [1].
