Problem

As medical technology saves more lives both young and old, those with chronic conditions are in need of more care than ever before. More people are finding themselves in the position of informal caregiver, professional caregivers are dealing with an increased load of patients, and both are left with little to no help from others and a lack of purpose-built tools.

Characteristics

Caregiver burden, which plagues nearly 50% of informal caregivers (Soh et al., 2025), is primarily linked to insufficient resources and responsibility conflict (Liu et al., 2020).

More than 1 in 5 Americans are caregivers, having provided care to an adult or child with special needs within the past year (AARP, 2020).

For informal caregivers of family dealing with chronic or rare conditions, proactive planning was the greatest reducer of stress (Zajdel et al., 2023).

More than half of all caregivers need more information and help with care topics such as managing stress and keeping their patient safe (AARP, 2020).

Solution

Sure-Step is a mobile and desktop application designed to streamline and support the daily workflows of both informal and professional caregivers. The platform provides a centralized space for managing care-related responsibilities, including task scheduling, appointment tracking, medication reminders, and documentation of patient needs.

To address the widespread issue of caregiver burden, Sure-Step emphasizes organization, clarity, and proactive planning. Users are able to break down complex caregiving responsibilities into manageable tasks, reducing stress caused by responsibility conflict and lack of structure. The system also enables caregivers to anticipate upcoming needs through reminders and planning tools, aligning with research showing that proactive planning significantly reduces caregiver stress.

In addition, Sure-Step improves access to essential information and resources by offering guided support, educational content, and structured care logs. This helps caregivers make informed decisions and feel more confident in their roles, especially those without formal training.

The platform is designed to scale across different caregiving scenarios, supporting individual caregivers managing family members as well as professionals coordinating care for multiple patients. By combining organization tools, resource access, and workflow management into a single system, Sure-Step reduces cognitive overload and empowers caregivers to provide more effective and consistent care
