Caregiver Platform

Support that keeps caregivers one step ahead

Sure-Step is a mobile and desktop application that helps informal and professional caregivers plan, organize, and coordinate the daily work of caring for someone else.

The Problem

As medical technology saves more lives — both young and old — people living with chronic conditions need more ongoing care than ever before. More Americans are stepping into the role of informal caregiver, while professional caregivers are managing heavier patient loads. Both groups often operate with limited external support and a shortage of purpose-built tools.

Who This Affects

Caregiver burden is widespread, and the data makes clear how much opportunity there is to reduce stress through better tools, clearer information, and proactive planning.

~50% of informal caregivers experience caregiver burden, driven by insufficient resources and responsibility conflict.
(Soh et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2020)
1 in 5 Americans provided care to an adult or child with special needs in the past year.
(AARP, 2020)
#1 stress reducer for family caregivers of people with chronic or rare conditions is proactive planning.
(Zajdel et al., 2023)
50%+ of caregivers say they need more information and help with topics like managing stress and keeping their patient safe.
(AARP, 2020)

Our Solution

Sure-Step is designed to streamline and support the daily workflows of both informal and professional caregivers. It provides a centralized space for managing care-related responsibilities: task scheduling, appointment tracking, medication reminders, and documentation of patient needs.

To address caregiver burden directly, Sure-Step emphasizes organization, clarity, and proactive planning. Complex responsibilities are broken down into manageable tasks, reducing the stress caused by responsibility conflict and lack of structure. Reminders and planning tools help users anticipate upcoming needs, aligning with research that shows proactive planning significantly reduces caregiver stress.

Sure-Step also improves access to essential information through guided support, educational content, and structured care logs, helping caregivers make informed decisions and feel more confident in their roles, especially those without formal training.

The platform is designed to scale across caregiving scenarios: individual caregivers managing a family member, or professionals coordinating care across many patients. By combining organization tools, resource access, and workflow management into a single system, Sure-Step reduces cognitive overload and empowers caregivers to provide more effective, consistent care.
